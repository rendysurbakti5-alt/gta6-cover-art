ViceFrame is a cover art studio that runs entirely in the browser: drop in your own photos, arrange them across a multi-panel poster layout, grade the whole piece in a Vice City sunset or neon palette, letter it with a bold caption, and export a print-ready PNG. Six layouts (Hero, Quad, Triptych, Cinema Strip, Diagonal, Hero Frame) and four colour grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Hour, Noir) combine freely; every panel's photo can be panned and zoomed independently, one photo can fill every panel or each panel can carry its own, and exports come in the three common post sizes — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Photos are read locally and never uploaded; the app is plain static files with no build step and works fully offline. The artwork, lettering and branding are original — drawn with canvas gradients, layered strokes and a star badge, in the spirit of the genre rather than copied from any game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. The studio opens with two sample photos already arranged into a cover — add your own photos, pick a layout and grade, edit the caption, and press Export PNG.
